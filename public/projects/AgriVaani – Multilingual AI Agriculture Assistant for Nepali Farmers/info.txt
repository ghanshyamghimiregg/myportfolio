Developed AgriVaani, a multilingual agriculture assistant available in Nepali, English, Maithili, and Bhojpuri. It enables farmers to onboard via voice in their own language, creating detailed profiles with AI from their introductions. Farmers can then ask agriculture-related questions (via text, image, or voice) and receive personalized answers based on their unique profiles. The app also provides location-based weather updates, daily market prices for crops, and an agriculture news feed automatically translated into their chosen language.
