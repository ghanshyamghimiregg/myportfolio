Built a real-time student behavior recognition system using skeleton-based GCN. My role focused on data annotation and model training — labeling the SCB dataset with both bounding boxes and 17 COCO-format keypoints per person, forming full-body skeleton graphs (joints as nodes, bones as edges) for 8 behavior classes. Trained PCCTR-GCN on 30-frame temporal sequences with pose correction for occluded joints, improving sleeping detection from 65% to 85% accuracy and achieving 30+ FPS in real time.
